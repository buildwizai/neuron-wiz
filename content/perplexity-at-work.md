## Getting Started: Perplexity as Your Unified AI Platform
* Core Tools/Features
- Comet (AI browser for research, tasks, complex workflows)
- Labs (Creation studio for presentations, dashboards, campaigns)
- Research (Deep web and file research agent, cited reports)

- Email Assistant (Manage inbox, draft replies, handle tasks)

## Part 1: Block Distractions (Reclaim Focus)
* Goal: Eliminate friction that fragments attention
- Delegate administrative overhead
- Restore capacity for sustained curiosity
* 3 Ways to Reclaim Focus
- Perplexity as a Personal Assistant (Handle everyday tasks)
- Get rid of constant switching (Research happens in less tabs via Comet)
- Automate your repetitive work (Turn multi-step jobs into simple prompts)
* Comet: Assistant vs Agent
- Comet Assistant (Smart helper for reading, understanding, answering)
- Capabilities
-- Summarizing (Articles, emails, web pages)
-- Conversational Help (Ask questions by talking or typing)
-- Research Support (Translations, explanations, fact-checks)
-- Tab Awareness (Can see multiple open tabs)
- Comet Agent (The doer, takes care of tasks for you)
- Capabilities
-- Finding flights, scheduling meetings, filling forms
-- Managing email subscriptions, handling purchases
-- Connecting services (Gmail, Calendar, Google Drive)
-- Handles entire multi-step workflows
* Perplexity Email Assistant
- Operates as intelligent layer over existing email system (Gmail/Outlook)
- Features
-- Smart Auto-Labeling (To Respond, FYI, Notifications)
-- Meeting Scheduler (Proposes time slots, sends invites)
-- Voice-Matched Reply Generation (Learns communication style)
* Eliminate Context Switching
- Perplexity simplifies workflow by bringing tasks/research together
- Practical Applications
-- Summarization (Email, industry news)
-- Get Updates from All of your Software Tools (Salesforce, Linear, Github, Notion)
-- Meeting Prep (Current KPIs, initiative updates, prospect history)
-- Document and Information Retrieval (Unified search across platforms)
* Automate Recurring Workflows
- Comet Shortcuts (Easy commands using "/")
-- Example: Client follow-up, Schedule team meeting, Weekly status
- Perplexity Tasks (Scheduled research queries)
-- Example: Create personalized daily newsletter, Conduct weekly competitive analysis, New AI regulation tracking
