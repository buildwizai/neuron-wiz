# Perplexity at Work: A Guide to Getting More Done

## Introduction

- Working Smarter with AI
  - Reality is often messy with multiple subscriptions, scattered apps, and added complexity
- Productive Work Unfolds in Three Stages
  - Block Distractions to reclaim focus and time
  - Scale Yourself to extend individual capabilities as a force multiplier
  - Get Results by channeling enhanced capability toward measurable outcomes

## Getting Started: Perplexity as Your Unified AI Platform

- Core Tools and Features
  - Comet as the AI browser for research, tasks, and complex workflows
  - Labs as the creation studio for presentations, dashboards, and campaigns
  - Research as the deep web and file research agent that produces cited reports
  - Spaces to keep research, notes, and context organized
  - Email Assistant to manage the inbox, draft replies, and handle tasks
- Perplexity Roles
  - Thinking Partner for fast, reliable information on market analysis, technical details, and research
  - Working Partner to produce deliverables such as slides, dashboards, marketing assets, and prototypes
- Prompting for Work Tips
  - Start by sharing your goals instead of treating Perplexity like a search engine
  - Be specific about the desired outcome and document type
  - Use your own context via the `@tab` feature to reference pages
  - Structure multi-step workflows by breaking requests into sequential steps

## Part 1: Block Distractions (Reclaim Focus)

- Goal: Eliminate friction that fragments attention
  - Delegate administrative overhead
  - Restore capacity for sustained curiosity
- Three Ways to Reclaim Focus
  - Perplexity as a Personal Assistant to handle everyday tasks
  - Reduce context switching by keeping research in fewer tabs through Comet
  - Automate repetitive work and turn multi-step jobs into simple prompts
- Comet: Assistant vs. Agent
  - Comet Assistant acts as a smart helper for reading, understanding, and answering
    - Summarizes articles, emails, and web pages
    - Provides conversational help through voice or text
    - Supports research by handling translations, explanations, and fact-checks
    - Maintains tab awareness to reference multiple open pages
  - Comet Agent executes tasks end-to-end
    - Finds flights, schedules meetings, and fills forms
    - Manages email subscriptions and handles purchases
    - Connects services such as Gmail, Calendar, and Google Drive
    - Orchestrates entire multi-step workflows
- Perplexity Email Assistant
  - Operates as an intelligent layer over Gmail or Outlook
  - Offers smart auto-labeling with buckets like To Respond, FYI, and Notifications
  - Provides a meeting scheduler that proposes time slots and sends invites
  - Generates voice-matched replies by learning your communication style
- Eliminate Context Switching
  - Simplify workflows by bringing tasks and research together in one place
  - Apply practical scenarios such as:
    - Summarizing email and industry news
    - Pulling updates from tools like Salesforce, Linear, GitHub, and Notion
    - Preparing for meetings with current KPIs, initiative updates, and prospect history
    - Retrieving documents and information via unified search across platforms
- Automate Recurring Workflows
  - Use Comet Shortcuts as quick commands invoked with `/`
    - Examples include client follow-up, scheduling team meetings, and weekly status updates
  - Schedule Perplexity Tasks as recurring research queries
    - Examples include creating a personalized daily newsletter, conducting weekly competitive analysis, and tracking new AI regulations

## Part 2: Scale Yourself (Amplify Your Natural Curiosity)

- Goal: Work at the scale of your actual curiosity
  - Conduct research with the depth of a dedicated team
  - Create professional deliverables that reflect complete thinking
- Four Essential Approaches to Scaling
  - Build AI into existing workflows
    - Integrate Perplexity into regular processes by thinking in systems instead of isolated tasks
    - Design end-to-end workflows using Labs and Comet together
  - Advance research beyond upgraded search engines
    - Apply Perplexity to:
      - Market intelligence and competitive analysis
      - Strategic planning to validate assumptions
      - Technology and industry assessments
      - Data analysis such as investigating Q3 sales trends
      - Investment and partnership evaluations
      - Product development research to uncover unmet needs
      - Regulatory compliance landscape reviews
      - Customer and market validation
  - Accelerate content creation
    - Rely on Labs to manage formatting, design, and production details
    - Incorporate image and video generation when needed
    - Produce assets such as:
      - Board presentations built from rough notes
      - Strategy documents with professional formatting
      - Client proposals with comprehensive detail
      - Internal updates for projects and initiatives
      - Training materials like onboarding guides
  - Solve complex problems with expert-level insight
    - Tackle scenarios such as:
      - Strategic business challenges like evaluating a freemium transition
      - Cross-industry solution adaptation, e.g., applying manufacturing ideas to capacity planning
      - Financial and investment analysis like buy-versus-build decisions
      - Operational efficiency improvements informed by project management methodologies
      - Market entry and expansion feasibility studies
- Scale Research with Perplexity Spaces
  - Use dedicated environments to keep content consistent
  - Leverage features such as:
    - Custom instructions that define style, terminology, and quality expectations
    - Brand consistency by referencing sample materials like presentations or reports
  - Start from template examples including:
    - Prospect Researcher, which generates detailed reports covering company overview, financial profile, decision makers, technology, and strategic opportunities
    - Product Enablement Hub, which is trained on internal documents to provide confident, customer-ready responses

## Part 3: Get Results (Turn Curiosity Into Career Advancement)

- Goal: Channel enhanced bandwidth toward outcomes that are recognized and valued
  - Focus attention on strategic thinking related to revenue, promotions, and key initiatives
- Four Critical Areas to Demonstrate Value
  - Performance Reviews and Professional Development
    - Role of Perplexity:
      - Comet analyzes work data such as projects, messages, and calendar activity
      - Labs assembles professional reports
    - Apply the insights to:
      - Understand work patterns and productivity
      - Assess skill development progress
      - Identify strengths and points of differentiation
    - Generate reports that support comprehensive performance reviews and development plans
  - Lead Generation and Business Development
    - Use Perplexity to:
      - Create lead generation dashboards that identify companies, funding status, and contacts
      - Analyze prospect companies for financials, initiatives, and challenges
      - Map the competitive landscape, including existing vendors and budget allocation
      - Build targeted outreach with personalized messaging, custom proposals, and case studies
      - Support strategic development such as partnership research and market entry strategy
  - Sales and Deal Closing
    - Rely on Labs to produce professional, prospect-specific materials
    - Example workflow: create an interactive win-loss analysis dashboard inside Labs
  - Project Delivery and Execution
    - Apply Perplexity to keep initiatives on track, coordinate workstreams, and document progress
