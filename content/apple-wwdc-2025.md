## Apple Intelligence
*   **Foundational Intelligence Journey**
    *   Began last year, focused on helpful & relevant intelligence
    *   Existing features:
        *   Writing Tools (virtually anywhere you type)
        *   Genmoji & Image Playground (express yourself)
        *   Clean Up (remove photo distractions)
        *   Visual Intelligence (explore surroundings)
        *   Natural language search for photos/videos
        *   Memory movie creation from description
        *   Summarize emails, notifications, notes
        *   Smart Reply for crafting responses
    *   **Privacy in AI:** Private Cloud Compute
        *   Extends iPhone privacy to cloud
        *   No one, not even Apple, can access your data
    *   Siri Enhancements:
        *   More natural & helpful
        *   Continued work to make Siri even more personal (coming next year)
    *   Expansion & Integration:
        *   Expanding supported languages
        *   More capable & efficient generative models
        *   Integrated across iPhone, Apple Watch, Apple Vision Pro, Mac, iPad
*   **New Foundation Models Framework**
    *   Opens access for any app to on-device large language model
    *   Benefits: Powerful, fast, private, available offline
    *   Ignites new wave of intelligent app experiences
    *   Examples:
        *   Kahoot! personalized quiz from notes (no cloud API costs)
        *   AllTrails off-grid hike suggestions based on mood
    *   Developers can build smart, offline, privacy-protecting experiences
## Universal Design Language
*   **A "Once per decade" Project**
    *   Broadest design update ever
    *   Last major redesign was iOS 7
    *   Driven by powerful Apple silicon & vital devices
*   **Goal & Principles**
    *   Beautiful, joyful, delightful user experience
    *   More personal, greater focus on content
    *   Instantly familiar feel
    *   Unified design across platforms for harmonious experience
    *   Inspired by physicality & richness of visionOS
*   **Introducing Liquid Glass Material**
    *   Entirely new expressive material
    *   Optical qualities of glass with Apple-achieved fluidity
    *   Transforms depending on content or context
    *   Brings clarity to navigation & controls
    *   Refracts light, dynamically reacts to movement (specular highlights)
    *   Responds in real time to content & input
    *   Elements redesigned to fit rounded hardware corners (greater harmony)
    *   Translucent, color informed by content, adapts to light/dark
    *   Dynamically morphs for more options or view changes
*   **Dynamic Nature Across Experiences**
    *   Alerts appear from where you tap
    *   Context menus expand into scannable lists
    *   Tab bars shrink on scroll, instantly return on scroll up
    *   More intuitive navigation (apps, Lock Screen, notifications, Control Center)
    *   App icons crafted with multiple Liquid Glass layers (light, dark, tints, clear look)
    *   Extends to Dock & widgets for personalization
*   **Impact:** Blurs lines between hardware & software, creates delightful & familiar experience
*   **Unified Version Numbers:** All platforms (iOS, macOS, watchOS, tvOS, visionOS, iPadOS) will be version 26

## iPadOS 26
*   **Core Enhancements**
    *   Giant release, beautiful new design
    *   Lock Screen (new time layout), Home Screen (redesigned icons, widgets), Apple TV app (content focus)
    *   Apple Intelligence integrated: Live Translation, supercharge shortcuts
    *   Includes iOS/macOS features: Messages (polls, group chats), new Phone app, new Games app
*   **Multitasking: New Windowing System**
    *   Designed for unique qualities of iPad, maintains simplicity
    *   Apps initially open full screen
    *   Grab handle (bottom right) to fluidly resize apps into windows
    *   Remembers size & position of windows
    *   Precise & responsive pointer (touch or trackpad)
    *   New buttons (top left) expand to familiar controls (close, minimize, tap app icon to bring back)
    *   Tiling: Flick windows towards edges to tile, grabber to resize both simultaneously
    *   More tiling options (e.g., quarters)
    *   Open even more windows
    *   Swipe home to peek at Home Screen, tap app to open with previous windows
    *   Exposé: Swipe up & hold to spread out windows, tap to bring to front
    *   Swipe home/up to minimize everything, start fresh
    *   Menu bar on iPad: Always available, familiar, clear labeled options
    *   Available on every iPad (Pro, Air, mini, basic iPad)
    *   Works with Stage Manager & external displays
*   **Files & Productivity**
    *   **Supercharged Files App:**
        *   Updated list view (resizable columns, collapsible folders)
        *   Folder customization (colors, icons) sync across devices
        *   Choose & set default app to open file (e.g., Photoshop, VSCO, Darkroom)
    *   Folders in the Dock: Drag any folder, fans out showing files, easy drag in/out
    *   **Preview App (from macOS):** Dedicated app to view & edit PDFs
        *   Apple Pencil Markup, Autofill

    *   Audio Input Selector: Specify which microphone to use (per app, web support)
    *   Voice Isolation: Block ambient sounds, keep voices clear & crisp (any app)
    *   Record studio-quality vocals directly with AirPods (improved tonal balance/timbre)
    *   AirPods: Start/stop video recording with press & hold

        *   Produce amazing content with high-quality recordings right from iPad

        *   Captures local high-quality audio & video
