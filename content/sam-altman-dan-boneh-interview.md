# AI and its Future: Open Questions, Career, and Impact
## Open Questions and Research Frontiers
- Deep learning thrust continues
- **Data Efficiency is a huge problem**
-	Systems need a lot of data points to generalize
-	Humans learn way more from each byte of data
- Separating concepts of pre-training and RL
- Building systems that can autonomously discover new science
- Rate of progress is still super steep
## The Nature of Intelligence
- Systems are already vastly superhuman at some things
- Goal is to build something different, not just replicate humans
- AI as another contributor to the "scaffolding" of civilization
-	Accumulated knowledge, artifacts, and tool chain
## Career Choice: Focus on AI
- It is the best field to go into right now
- It is the most important trend of this generation
- Students have the opportunity for this to be the most important work they ever touch
## Future Integration and Hype Cycle
- Hope that AI becomes ubiquitous in 5-10 years
- AI will be way more powerful and integrated into everything
- People will expect every product and service to be smart
- Expectation is that "AI company" will stop being a meaningful distinction
## Preparing for an AI World
- Most important skill will be using AI to do amazing things
-	Not necessarily being able to build the AI itself
- AI will be assumed to work, like a calculator
- Encourage kids to be resilient, adaptable, curious, and creative
## AI Security
- One of the best areas for study
- AI safety problems will be recast as AI security problems
- Security problems are growing as models become more capable
- Important sub-areas:
-	Adversary robustness
-	Personalization vs. Data Exfiltration
-		Challenge: Highly personalized models connected to external services
-		Risk: Preventing models from sharing private, sensitive data
- **AI for Security**
-	AI is amazing at finding bugs in software
-	Potential for superhuman AI security analysts
-	Can be used for securing systems and cyber attacks
## Impact on Software Development and Education
- **Software Development**
-	Will look very different in 2-10 years
-	Will mostly involve talking to the computer (pseudo code or English)
-	Developers will be much more productive
-	Future process: Describe desired software; system writes and tests code
-	Expect software engineering agents to manage repositories and write tests
- **CS Education**
-	Curriculum needs to change significantly
-	Need to focus on how to write code in a world of software engineering agents
-	Value of learning complex foundational skills (OS, compilers) may shift
-		Focus will move toward general metalearning and intellectual curiosity
-		Fewer people will need to understand the depths of creation
## Programming Languages
- Human-compatible languages (Java, Go, Rust) will remain important
- Readability and editability are highly valuable
- If AI generates perfect code, requirements might change
## AI Architecture and Research Location
- **Architecture**
-	Current architectures (DNNs, Transformers) are not the end of the roadmap
-	Gigantic discoveries are still to be made
-	Expect architectures to shift again
- **Source of Innovation**
-	New ideas can come from anywhere (academia, industry, open source)
-	Takes a small group of talented people pushing a new direction
## Energy Consumption
- Comparisons between human and AI consumption are often unfair
-	Incorrect comparison: Human inference vs. ML training cost
-	Fair comparison: Watts per token generated (inference to inference)
- Expect massive future energy efficiency gains (100x or more)
-	New hardware architectures (e.g., mixing storage and compute)
-	New hardware substrates (e.g., optical computing)
-	Better algorithms
-	Engineering solutions (e.g., better cooling)
## General Career Advice
- Focus on AI
- Find the smartest cluster of optimistic people working on something interesting
- Hang around them a lot
- Work on interesting problems and run a tight feedback loop
