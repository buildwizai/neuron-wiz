# Anthropic Economic Index: Uneven AI Adoption
## Introduction & Key Findings
- **Unprecedented Adoption Speed**
  - Faster than electricity, PCs, and the internet
- **Hallmark of Early Adoption: Concentration**
  - Geographic regions
  - Specific tasks within firms
- **Report Focus**
  - Geographic analysis of Claude.ai
  - Enterprise API use

## Chapter 1: Claude.ai Usage Over Time
- **Changing Task Patterns (over 8 months)**
  - **Dominant:** Coding (36%)
  - **Rising:** Education (9.3% -> 12.4%), Science (6.3% -> 7.2%)
  - **Falling:** Business & Finance, Management
- **Impact of New Features**
  - Web search -> More electronic source searching
  - Research mode -> More internet-based research
- **Shift Towards Code Creation**
  - Program creation more than doubled (+4.5pp)
  - Debugging decreased (-2.9pp)
  - Suggests increased model reliability
- **Accelerating Directive Automation**
- **Automation vs. Augmentation**
  - **Automation (Task Completion):** Directive, Feedback Loops
  - **Augmentation (Collaboration):** Learning, Task Iteration, Validation
- **Trend:** Directive use jumped from 27% to 39%
- **Potential Causes**
  - Improved model capabilities
  - User learning-by-doing
- **Labor Market Implications**
  - Worker displacement vs. higher wages for adaptable workers

## Chapter 2: The Geography of AI Adoption
- **Anthropic AI Usage Index (AUI)**
  - Measures usage relative to working-age population
- **Global Diffusion**
  - **Concentration:** Geographically concentrated in high-income countries
  - **Per Capita (AUI) Leaders:** Israel, Singapore, Australia
  - **Emerging Economies:** Lower usage (India, Indonesia, Nigeria)
  - **Strong Correlation:** AUI and GDP per capita
- **US Diffusion**
  - **Total Usage Leaders:** California, New York, Texas
  - **Per Capita (AUI) Leaders:** DC, Utah
  - Weaker correlation with income than globally
- **Task Usage Patterns**
  - **High-Adoption Countries:** More diverse tasks (education, science, business)
  - **Low-Adoption Countries:** Dominated by coding (e.g., India)
  - **Local Economic Specialization**
    - CA: IT
    - FL: Financial services
    - DC: Document editing, career assistance
- **Human-AI Collaboration Patterns**
  - **High-AUI Countries:** More augmentation (collaborative)
  - **Low-AUI Countries:** More automation (delegation)

## Chapter 3: Enterprise AI Deployment (API)
- **Context: Early Stages of Business Adoption**
  - Growing but uneven (9.7% of US firms)
- **API vs. Claude.ai Usage**
  - **API Higher:** Coding, Office/Admin tasks
  - **Claude.ai Higher:** Education, Writing tasks
- **Key Findings on API Usage**
  - **Automation Dominant:** 77% of business use is automation
  - **Price Insensitivity:** Higher-cost tasks have higher usage; capabilities & value matter more
  - **Concentrated Use:** Follows a power law, driven by AI-task fit
  - **Context is a Bottleneck**
    - Complex tasks require more contextual input
    - Diminishing returns (1% input increase -> 0.38% output increase)
    - Data organization is a barrier to adoption
- **Labor Market Implications**
  - Productivity gains via systematic automation
  - Disruption for some roles
  - Increased value for workers with tacit knowledge

## Conclusion & Open Data
- **Summary: AI adoption is uneven & concentrated**
  - Tasks, geography, and enterprise vs. consumer
- **Risk of Divergence**
  - Widening economic inequality
- **Future Research**
  - Open-sourcing data to catalyze research
  - Investigate labor market impacts
  - Understand determinants of adoption
