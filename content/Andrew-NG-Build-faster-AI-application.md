# Building Startups Faster with AI
## AI Stack & Opportunities
*   Lowest Level: Semiconductor companies
*   Clouds / Hyperscalers
*   AI Foundation Model companies
*   **Application Layer**
    *   Biggest opportunities
    *   Generates most revenue
## Rise of Agentic AI
*   Most important tech trend
*   **Agentic Workflows**
    *   Iterative process (thinking, research, revision)
    *   Delivers better work product (though slower)
    *   Crucial for complex tasks (compliance, medical diagnosis, legal)
    *   Enabled by new Agentic Orchestration Layer
## Best Practices for Startup Speed
*   **Work on Concrete Ideas**
    *   Specified enough for engineers to build
    *   Vague ideas hinder speed
    *   Provide clear direction for rapid building, validation, or falsification
    *   Requires subject matter expertise & long-term thinking
    *   Expert gut instinct is faster than data for decisions
    *   Pursue one clear hypothesis; pivot quickly if disproven
    *   Frequent pivoting indicates weak knowledge base
*   **Rapid Engineering with AI Coding Assistance**
    *   **Build-Feedback Loop**
        *   Engineer software -> User feedback -> Tweak -> Repeat
    *   AI makes engineering feasible and cheaper
    *   **Software Types**
        *   **Quick & Dirty Prototypes**
            *   10x faster (or more) with AI
            *   Less integration, lower requirements (reliability, security)
            *   "Write insecure code" for testing (secure before shipping)
            *   Enables building 20 prototypes to find what works
        *   **Production Quality Code**
            *   30-50% faster with AI
    *   **Evolution of AI Coding Tools**
        *   Code autocomplete (GitHub Copilot)
        *   AI-enabled IDEs
        *   Highly agentic coding assistants (o3, Cloud Codeex)
    *   **Impact on Engineering**
        *   Developer productivity keeps growing
        *   Code is less valuable artifact, easier to rebuild
        *   Software architecture decisions becoming more "two-way doors"
    *   **Empowering Everyone to Code**
        *   AI makes coding easier, more people should learn
        *   Empower all job roles (CFO, HR, recruiters, front desk)
        *   Ability to tell computer what you want is key future skill
        *   Steering AI to code is the best way
*   **Speed Up Product Feedback**
    *   Product management becoming the bottleneck
    *   Shift in PM to Engineer ratio (more PMs relative to engineers)
    *   PMs who can code or engineers with product instincts excel
    *   **Tactics for Rapid Feedback**
        *   **Fastest/Less Accurate**
            *   Self-assessment/gut (if expert)
            *   Ask 3 friends/teammates
            *   Ask 3-10 strangers (e.g., in public places)
        *   **Slower/More Accurate**
            *   Send prototypes to 100 testers
            *   Prototype to more users
            *   AB testing (now one of the slowest)
        *   **Learning from Data**
            *   Use data to hone instincts for faster, high-quality decisions
*   **Understand AI (Stay on Top of Technology)**
    *   Knowledge of AI is not widespread, provides advantage
    *   Right technical decisions save significant time
    *   Access to numerous GenAI building blocks (prompting, evals, RAG, fine-tuning)
    *   Combining blocks leads to exponential possibilities
    *   Architect software for easy switching between building block providers
## The Future with AI
*   **Humans' Role**
    *   Most powerful people can make computers do exactly what they want
*   **Hype vs. Reality**
    *   Dangerous hype narratives are distortions (human extinction, job loss, only nuclear power for AI)
    *   **Responsible AI**: Safety is how we apply technology, not inherent to technology itself
    *   Avoid sensationalizing lab experiments
*   **Building Business in AI Era**
    *   Primary focus: Build a product users love
    *   Moats often evolve; brand/momentum can be defensible
    *   Many opportunities ("white space") at the application layer
*   **Education in AI**
    *   Likely to be hyper-personalized
    *   Extensive experimentation ongoing; end state unclear
    *   Mapping complex human workflows to agentic workflows is still developing
*   **Societal Downsides & Responsibility**
    *   Don't build products that don't make people better off (kill projects on ethical grounds)
    *   Empower everyone with AI knowledge to prevent inequality
    *   **Danger of Gatekeepers**: Hype used to push regulations harming open source/open weight models
    *   Protecting open source is crucial for innovation and knowledge diffusion
