# How People Use ChatGPT (NBER Working Paper 34255)
## Introduction & Growth
- **Study Period:** Nov 2022 - July 2025
- **Rapid Adoption**
  - By July 2025:
  - ~700 million weekly active users
  - ~10% of global adult population
  - >2.5 billion messages per day
- **Growth Trends**
  - Message volume grew by over 5x from July 2024 to July 2025
  - Growth from both new and existing user cohorts
  - Usage consistently grows within each cohort over time

## Methodology: Data & Privacy
- **Data Sources**
  - **Growth Dataset:** Daily message volumes and basic demographics
  - **Classified Messages:** Random sample of ~1 million de-identified messages
  - **Employment Dataset:** Aggregated data for a subset of users
- **Privacy-Preserving Approach**
  - **No human observation of user messages**
  - **Automated Classification Pipeline**
  - PII (Personally Identifiable Information) scrubbing
  - LLM-based classifiers categorize message content
  - **Secure Data Clean Room**
    - For employment data analysis
    - Only pre-approved aggregate queries allowed
    - Strict aggregation limits (minimum 100 users per group)

## How ChatGPT is Used
- **Work vs. Non-Work Usage**
  - Non-work messages grew faster than work-related messages
  - **Share of non-work messages increased from 53% (June 2024) to >70% (June 2025)**
- **Conversation Topics**
  - **Top 3 (nearly 80% of all conversations)**
  - **Practical Guidance (29%):** How-to advice, tutoring, creative ideation
  - **Writing (24%):** Declined from 36% in July 2024
  - **Seeking Information (24%):** Grew from 14% in July 2024
  - **Writing Breakdown**
    - **Most common work task (40% of work messages)**
    - Two-thirds involve modifying user-provided text (editing, translating, summarizing)
    - One-third involves generating novel text
  - **Other Topics**
    - **Technical Help (5%):** Declined from 12%, includes Computer Programming (4.2%)
    - **Multimedia (7%):** Grew after image generation release
    - **Self-Expression (small share):** Relationships (1.9%), Games/Role Play (0.4%)
- **User Intent: Asking, Doing, Expressing**
  - **Asking (49%):** Seeking information/advice for decision-making
  - **Doing (40%):** Requesting a direct output (e.g., writing an email, code)
  - **Expressing (11%):** Non-task-oriented conversation
- **Trends**
  - "Asking" and "Expressing" have grown faster than "Doing"
  - **At Work:** "Doing" is dominant (56%), primarily for Writing tasks
- **O*NET Work Activities**
  - **Top 2 functions account for ~81% of work-related messages:**
    1. Obtaining, documenting, and interpreting information
    2. Making decisions, giving advice, solving problems, thinking creatively
  - **Remarkable similarity in top activities across different occupations**
- **Interaction Quality**
  - "Good" interactions are 4x more common than "Bad" and grew faster
  - **Highest Rated:** Self-Expression, Asking
  - **Lowest Rated:** Multimedia, Technical Help

## Who Uses ChatGPT (Demographics)
- **Gender**
  - **Gender gap has closed over time**
  - Early adopters: ~80% masculine names
  - June 2025: Slightly more users with feminine names
  - **Topic Differences**
    - Feminine names: More Writing, Practical Guidance
    - Masculine names: More Technical Help, Seeking Information, Multimedia
- **Age**
  - **Nearly half of messages from users under 26**
  - Work-related use increases with age (except for users 66+)
- **Country**
  - **Disproportionately faster growth in low- and middle-income countries**
- **Education**
  - **Highly educated users are more likely to use ChatGPT for work**
  - Users with graduate degrees are more likely to use it for "Asking"
- **Occupation**
  - **Higher work-related usage in professional and technical jobs** (e.g., computer, management)
  - **Professionals use it more for "Asking" at work**
  - **Topic usage aligns with job tasks:**
    - Writing common in Management/Business
    - Technical Help common in Computer-related jobs

## Conclusion: Key Findings
- **1. Non-work usage dominates (70%) and is growing faster**
- **2. Top 3 topics (Practical Guidance, Writing, Seeking Information) account for ~78% of use**
- **3. Writing is the most common work-related use case (42%)**
- **4. "Asking" (49%) is more common than "Doing" (40%), growing faster, and rated higher**
- **5. Gender gap in usage has closed**
- **6. Younger users (<26) are the most active group**
- **7. Usage has grown rapidly in low- and middle-income countries**
- **8. Educated professionals use ChatGPT more for work, especially for "Asking" tasks**
- **Economic Value:** Primarily from decision support in knowledge-intensive jobs
